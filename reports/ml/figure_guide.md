# Molecular PK figure guide

All figures are generated by `PYTHONPATH=src python -m pktk.ml.analyze`. Committed vector PDFs are in `reports/ml/figures/`; identical PDFs and 300-dpi PNGs are in `results/ml/figures/`. These depict conditional molecular-plus-known-dose prediction, not cross-dose extrapolation. No scientific source images are reproduced.

Recommended presentation sequence:

1. `01_ml_coverage.pdf`: audit-qualified compounds and assay-specific endpoint rows. The two tasks overlap in compound identity; do not add their compound counts to obtain a union.
2. `03_generalization_rat_oral_plasma_cmax.pdf`: mean family-weighted log10 MAE across declared seeds. Compound/scaffold diagnostics share publications; study-aware partitions do not. Test populations differ between designs, so this is not a controlled causal estimate of scaffold difficulty.
3. `02_models_rat_iv_plasma_clearance.pdf` and `02_models_rat_oral_plasma_cmax.pdf`: all methods, including mean and dose-only controls. Orange denotes graph models. Error bars are descriptive SD over overlapping splits, not confidence intervals or independent biological replication. Rankings are not deployment-model selection.
4. `04_predictions_*.pdf`: XGBoost and GINE on the first eligible declared strict seed (42), chosen by metadata eligibility, not prediction quality. Axes are log10 endpoint values in mL/min/kg for clearance and µg/mL for Cmax. Each point is an endpoint row; `n` denotes chemical families.
5. `07_uncertainty.pdf`: group coverage and selective risk for three-member ensembles on the fixed clearance split. Both coverage traces coincide. High coverage must be read with interval width: 90% radii are about 2.15–2.18 log10 units, roughly ×/÷141–153. Fourteen test groups and unverified shift exchangeability do not support a calibration guarantee.

Additional diagnostics: `05_similarity_*.pdf` pools displayed observations from overlapping strict split seeds; they are correlated. `06_learning_curve.pdf` uses nested whole training groups on one fixed split; the narrow vertical range and nonmonotonicity preclude scaling-law conclusions. `08_error_distributions.pdf` uses all strict split errors without outlier removal. Largest-error rows remain local in `results/ml/outliers.csv` for source review.

Model comparison, generalization, learning-curve and uncertainty PNGs were visually inspected for readable labels, legends, metric direction and units. Numerical tables and predictions are independently reconciled by `scripts/verify_ml_results.py`. All generated figures are checked for deterministic rendering by `scripts/verify_ml_reproducibility.py`.
