### Identity resources and access findings

- [ChEBI public API](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/chebi/tools), [documented schema](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/chebi/backend/api/docs/), [license and citation](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/chebi/about/): CC BY 4.0; Malik et al., *ChEBI: re-engineered for a sustainable future* (2025). Exact CAS/name evidence and full structure records are cached. Invalid source SMILES may be reconstructed from the same source's standard InChI only when RDKit reproduces the full expected InChIKey; that fallback is recorded explicitly.
- [UniChem API](https://chembl.gitbook.io/unichem/api/compound-search): current public POST compound-search endpoint maps exact InChIKeys to PubChem Compound IDs. Returned InChI must reproduce the query key. Multiple CID references remain a list; no single CID is forced. Legacy REST mapping returned 404, so the documented current API was used.
- Public ChEBI API crawl delay is respected. The first expansion queried 15 prioritized identifiers and left 238 `not_queried`. This historical limit is superseded by the full-registry EPA/ChEBI pass described below; full coverage does not mean every identity can be resolved unambiguously.
- PubChem direct REST requests and the ChEBI FTP listing were blocked by robots policies. No request to those blocked data paths was sent after the policy check.
- PK-DB legacy anonymous [statistics](https://pk-db.com/api/v1/statistics/) succeeded (global counts, not preclinical counts). [One-record metadata page](https://pk-db.com/api/v1/studies/?page=1&page_size=1) succeeded after reducing page size; it reports human study PKDB00954 with `licence=closed`. No measurements were imported. The newer beta endpoints failed TLS. Current [terms](https://matthiaskoenig.github.io/pkdb/terms-of-use/) and download authentication requirements remain applicable. Cite Grzegorzewski et al. (2021), doi:10.1093/nar/gkaa990 for PK-DB metadata use.

The CEBS contribution validates existing source experiments, not independent replication. The 18-candidate purposive audit is stratified by species/sex/route/matrix and includes 14 validated endpoint groups of doses, two matrix conflicts and two dose conflicts. That historical sample has now been expanded; see the current audit below. The observed 14/18 yield must not be extrapolated statistically to the entire candidate universe.

Full identity coverage is enabled (`identity_priority_limit: null`). EPA DSSTox exact DTXSID/CAS matches and ChEBI exact CAS/name evidence require full source-key validation; UniChem supplies optional PubChem cross-references. `httk 2.7.4`'s physical-property table was inspected as a possible bulk identity source, but it lacks SMILES/InChIKey fields. It contributes aliases only when CAS and DTXSID both match. Shared per-origin cache locks enforce crawl delays across concurrent collectors.

### EPA DSSTox bulk identity resolution

The [official EPA download catalog](https://www.epa.gov/comptox-tools/downloadable-computational-toxicology-data) links [DSSTox DOI 10.23645/epacomptox.5588566](https://doi.org/10.23645/epacomptox.5588566). Its public Figshare API release version 8 points to EPA's Clowder distribution. The **December 2025 CSV ZIP** (289,824,966 bytes, CC0) was downloaded from `https://clowder.edap-cluster.com/api/files/69529775e4b0731a616efc4b`. The master CSV is read once; split copies are not double-counted. Exact DTXSID and primary CAS must match, and RDKit must regenerate the full source InChIKey from the original SMILES. QSAR-ready and MS-ready normalized structures are never substituted. All matches/rejections and cross-source conflicts remain auditable. See `reports/dsstox_identity_manifest.json` for executed counts and checksum. PubChem CID cross-references are optional and queried for primary/candidate compounds, with existing caches reused for others.

## Current CEBS protocol expansion

The eight newly imported government source experiments are S0593 (oxybenzone), S0546 (2,4-D), S0558 (anthraquinone), S0571 (naphthalene), K02922 (4-methylimidazole), K07076 (2-methyltetrahydrofuran), K05097 (bromodichloromethane) and K10075 (PFOA). Official chemical-page citations, download URLs, versions and SHA-256 values are recorded in `cebs_expansion_manifest.json` and `configs/curation/cebs_expansion.json`. These are NTP/CEBS public government data; attribution and source exceptions are preserved, with no raw redistribution in Git. No separate dataset-specific license was found in these workbooks; the existing CEBS public-access attribution policy applies, without asserting that all third-party linked material has identical terms.

Verified addition: 91 source-reported dose groups, 31 comparable series, eight new validated compounds and eight studies. Twelve series contain both eligible AUC and observed Cmax. Species include rat, mouse and hamster; routes are oral and IV; whole blood and plasma remain separate. Source counts refer only to selected and checked experiments, not total CEBS holdings.

All 112 candidate groups were assessed, with source protocol material reviewed for 97 and 15 full protocols still unavailable/unreconciled. Reviewed material sometimes remains insufficient for validation. Source-copy overlap with CvTdb is explicit: these are newly validated studies, not newly independent replicas of the existing observations.

The [DTT TK guided-search catalog](https://cebs-ext.niehs.nih.gov/datasets/search/tk?casrn=50-06-6) was retrieved. It exposes compound, experiment, species/strain/sex, matrix, route, vehicle, model and annotated-download fields. Catalog discovery is not measurement validation. No new FDA/EMA regulatory dataset was imported in this run.

## Targeted research-stage addition

[CEBS K04003B: 8:2 fluorotelomer alcohol](https://cebs.niehs.nih.gov/cebs/test_article/678-39-7), NTP chemical collection DOI 10.22427/NTP-DATA-DTXSID7029904, supplies six parent-compound oral plasma Cmax groups: male rats at 12/24/48 mg/kg and female rats at 40/80/160 mg/kg. The original annotated workbook specifies Harlan Sprague-Dawley rats, single gavage, common 5 mL/kg Cremophor/ethanol/water formulation and sparse sampling. Parent/analyte/CAS and exact physical rows are checked. AUC values remain retained but ineligible because terminal-window/model comparability is unresolved. PFOA and 7:3-acid metabolites and tissue endpoints are excluded.

This adds **two validated Cmax series, one compound and one validated source study**. The K04003B endpoint study is not equated automatically with CvTdb's C20619 identifier or its metabolite candidate. It has its own document ID; any cross-source alias remains unresolved. Source bytes were already cached from the previous audit but had not been imported; this is a concrete new parent-specific extraction, not a retry of failed access. Public government source attribution and all source units are retained; raw/generated data remain untracked. Curation: `configs/curation/cebs_targeted.json`.
